Challenges: Polarity Match

Red + balls go into plus-shaped pits, blue − balls into minus-shaped pits. A pit only opens for its own sign, so you tilt the board to sort them.

  • 50 hand-made boards in five sets, from first steps to Master Sorter.
  • Three stars on every board: finish it, beat par, and do it first go.
  • A daily challenge every day, with bonus charge points and a streak to keep.
  • Coloured doors, bumpers, sticky floors and gravity that pulls to the middle.

What's inside

  • Five worlds, five kinds of gravity: Earth, Moon, Jupiter, Tidal and Orbit.
  • Walls, mazes, bumpers, sticky strips, breakable glass, teleporters, gates and moving blockers.
  • Six powers, won in Power Trials and filled from the Magnetic Vault.
  • On-screen controls, reduced motion, high contrast and larger text.
  • Plays offline. Hold the phone however you like, even lying in bed: the way you hold it when a level starts counts as level.

Made for every age

Polarity is designed for children and adults alike. There is no chat, no account and nothing to buy. Ads are child-safe and never personalised, never appear during play, and rewarded ads are always your choice.
